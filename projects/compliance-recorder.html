Compliance recording only has value if it is continuous. A capture that dies quietly at two in the morning is discovered when someone needs the material and finds a hole. This launcher starts all configured SDI channels, watches them, and restarts anything that stops. Recorded files are served over corporate user log-in web application. The problem

TOC SDI feeds needed continuous capture. Nobody was watching at the moment of failure, so the gap was found later, by someone who needed exactly that material.

Airing discrepancies cannot be validated due to lack of on-air recording.

The build

A launcher sits over four FFmpeg capture processes reading from the SDI inputs, and makes their state visible in one place.

  • Per-channel start and stop, with all four manageable from a single screen
  • Overlay text to properly label timestamp and channel being recorded.
  • Live preview per channel.
  • Segmented output files, keeping any single failure contained to one segment
  • All parameters configurable with auto purging to prevent local storage from running out of space.

The outcome

Compliance recording met using available resources and without needing to purchase enterprise level solution that does the same, effectively saving millions. On-air discrepancies that aired are now logged and outages are measured accurately, and disputes over material airing and proof of airing can now be easily accessed by authorized personnels.
