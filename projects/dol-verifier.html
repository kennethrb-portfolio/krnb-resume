The problem

Two systems hold a version of the same broadcast day. Traffic generates the commercial lineup; playout automation runs the log that actually reaches air. When the two drift apart — a spot dropped in the upload, a duration mismatch, a break that never made it across — nothing catches it. The discrepancy surfaces after the fact, once the spot has already missed its slot.

By then the cost is fixed: a make-good, an unhappy client, lost revenue on airtime that was already sold.

The build

The app puts both playlists in front of the operator in one pane and makes the comparison the whole job. It ingests the generated traffic log and the uploaded automation playlist, aligns them, and surfaces every point where they disagree.

  • Single-pane view of the full broadcast day, no scrolling between two systems
  • Per-row verification against the automation log, with mismatches flagged
  • Operator sign-off recorded per slot, so accountability is explicit
  • Authentication against corporate directory, with a local fallback for outages
  • Regenerates the source document with verification state carried through

The outcome

Verification moved from a reactive post-mortem to a routine pre-air step. Discrepancies that previously reached air are now caught and corrected while there is still time to fix them, protecting both the client commitment and the revenue attached to it.
